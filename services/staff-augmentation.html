Staff Augmentation

Scale Engineering Capacity On Your Terms

Hiring senior engineers takes months, and roadmaps rarely wait. RixlSoft staff augmentation gives you experienced developers, QA engineers, DevOps specialists and AI engineers who integrate directly into your team. They follow your processes, use your tools and report to your leads, while we handle sourcing, payroll, equipment, retention and continuous professional development behind the scenes.

Every engineer is screened through technical interviews, live coding and communication assessments before they meet you. You interview the shortlist, choose who joins, and scale the team up or down as priorities change, with clear monthly pricing and no long-term lock-in.

FAQs

Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about our Staff Augmentation services. Can't find an answer? Talk to our team.

Ask an Expert
How quickly can an augmented engineer start?
For common stacks such as React, Node.js, Python and cloud engineering, we typically present a shortlist within a few business days and aim to have an engineer onboarded within about two weeks. Niche skills or very senior architecture roles can take longer, and we share realistic timelines upfront before you commit to anything.
What determines the monthly cost of an engineer?
Pricing depends mainly on seniority, specialism and required time-zone overlap. A senior AI or DevOps engineer costs more than a mid-level frontend developer. Rates are quoted as a fixed monthly fee that covers salary, benefits, equipment, management and replacement guarantees, so there are no hidden recruitment or administration charges on top.
Who owns the code and intellectual property?
You do. Every engineer signs an NDA and an IP assignment agreement, and our master services agreement assigns all work product created during the engagement to your company. Engineers work within your repositories and accounts, so code, documentation and credentials remain under your control from the first commit onward.
Can I scale the team up or down?
Yes. Most engagements run on monthly terms with a short notice period, so you can add engineers when a new initiative starts and release them when the workload drops. We also help plan transitions, ensuring knowledge is documented and handed over before any engineer rolls off your project.
How do you manage performance and communication?
Engineers work inside your tools, including Slack, Jira and GitHub, and follow your rituals. A RixlSoft account manager runs regular check-ins with you, gathers feedback, and handles any performance or attendance concerns. If a fit is not working, we arrange a replacement and manage the handover at no extra cost.
