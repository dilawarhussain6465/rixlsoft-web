CRM Integration & Migration

Stop Copying Data Between Systems

Customer data scattered across spreadsheets, inboxes, accounting tools and an ageing CRM costs time and revenue. Sales reps re-key orders, finance chases missing details, and nobody trusts the reports. RixlSoft consolidates that data into a single CRM, whether Salesforce, HubSpot, Dynamics 365 or a custom build, and connects it to the systems around it so information is entered once and flows everywhere.

We treat migration as an engineering project: profiling source data, cleansing and deduplicating records, mapping relationships, running rehearsal loads and validating results before any cutover. Integrations are built with logging, retries and alerting, so the connected landscape stays accurate long after launch.

  • Migrations from spreadsheets and legacy CRMs
  • Data cleansing and deduplication
  • ERP, e-commerce and marketing integrations
  • Custom CRM builds when needed

FAQs

Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about our CRM Integration & Migration services. Can't find an answer? Talk to our team.

Ask an Expert
What affects the cost of a CRM migration or integration?
The main drivers are the number of source systems, data volume and quality, the complexity of relationships and history to preserve, and how many systems must integrate with the new CRM. Poor source data increases cleansing effort. After a data audit, we provide a fixed quote for migrations and a clear estimate for each integration.
How long does a CRM migration take?
A straightforward migration from spreadsheets or a small CRM can complete in three to five weeks. Larger migrations with several sources, heavy history, attachments and multiple integrations usually take two to four months. Most of the time goes into auditing, cleansing and rehearsal loads, which is what makes the final cutover smooth.
Will we lose data or history during migration?
Not if migration is planned properly. We map every field, preserve relationships, notes, activities and attachments where the target platform supports them, and run reconciliation reports comparing source and target. Anything that cannot be migrated directly is archived in an accessible format and agreed with you before cutover, never silently dropped.
Should we build a custom CRM or buy a platform?
Packaged platforms such as Salesforce, HubSpot or Dynamics 365 suit most businesses because they include mature features and ecosystems. A custom CRM makes sense when your workflows are highly specialised, per-user licences become prohibitive at scale, or the CRM is core to your product. We compare total cost of ownership before recommending either option.
How do you keep integrations reliable after go-live?
Each integration includes structured logging, alerting, automatic retries and error queues for records that fail validation. We provide dashboards showing sync health and offer support plans that cover monitoring, fixes and updates when connected platforms change their APIs, so your data stays consistent as your system landscape evolves.
